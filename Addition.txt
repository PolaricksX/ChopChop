The Tardy Cook:
Chef Marcus woke up early because he had to cook a very importent meal for the resturant.
He looked through his old recipe book and found a recipe for chicken pasta. It looked 
pretty easy, so he desided to make it.

First, Marcus got all the ingredeents out. He needed chicken, pasta, tomato sauce, garlic, 
cheese, and some spices. He started by boiling the pasta, but he forgot to add salt to the 
water. “Oh well,” he said, and kept going.

Next he cut the chicken into small peices and put them in a hot pan. The chicken started
to sizzle and smell really good. Marcus added garlic and pepper, but he accidently dropped 
way to much pepper into the pan.

After the chicken was cooked, he poured in the tomato sauce. He let it cook for about ten 
minutes while he waited for the pasta. When the pasta was finally done, he mixed everything 
together and added a bunch of cheese.

Marcus tasted the meal and smiled. It was a little to spicy, but still pretty good. He put 
the pasta on a plate and gave it to the customer.

The customer took one bite and said, “This is delicous!”
Marcus grinned. Maybe mistakes werent so bad after all.
